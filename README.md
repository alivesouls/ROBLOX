# Roblox Historical Asset Archive

A preservation archive collecting historical Roblox clothing assets — sourced from the official Roblox account, former Roblox staff members, Roblox's built-in catalog items, and independent creator **armitroner**. Built to support retro-era Roblox recreation projects (e.g. early-2010s client/UGC restoration) where original asset IDs and metadata are otherwise lost or unobtainable through the live catalog.

## Contents

### 1. Official Roblox & Former Staff Assets
T-Shirts and Shirts originally published under the official **Roblox** account and accounts belonging to former Roblox staff members. These represent early first-party catalog content that has since been delisted, replaced, or is no longer discoverable via standard catalog search.

- Asset type: T-Shirts, Shirts
- Source: Official Roblox account, ex-staff accounts
- Status: Historical / delisted

### 2. Built-in Roblox T-Shirts
The default set of built-in T-Shirt templates shipped with Roblox in earlier client versions, used as baseline/default avatar clothing before the modern catalog-driven onboarding flow.

- Asset type: T-Shirts
- Source: Built-in client defaults
- Status: Legacy default assets

### 3. armitroner's Assets
Full catalog output from the creator account **armitroner**, including T-Shirts, Shirts, and Pants.

- Asset type: T-Shirts, Shirts, Pants
- Source: armitroner (creator account)
- Status: Original creator catalog

## Purpose

This repository exists strictly for historical preservation and research/reference purposes tied to recreating early Roblox-era experiences. It does not host or redistribute any assets in violation of ownership — all entries are catalogued by ID/metadata sourced from publicly accessible Roblox data.

Respect original creator's work. Do not redistribute or claim as yours.
